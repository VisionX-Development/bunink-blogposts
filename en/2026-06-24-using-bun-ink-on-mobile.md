Writing rarely happens only at a desk. An idea strikes on the train, a paragraph gets better on the sofa, a note has to be captured on the go. **bun.ink** is designed precisely for those moments, so that the app remains perfectly usable on mobile devices too.

The focus is therefore not only on a large desktop interface, but on a **responsive, simple design**. The view should adapt to smartphones and tablets, controls should stay within reach, and the editor shouldn't feel heavier than it needs to. When you're writing, what matters is that as little as possible stands between the idea and the text.

## Browser-only instead of an app store hurdle

bun.ink deliberately takes a browser-only approach. That means you don't have to search for an app in an app store, install it and keep it up to date. You open bun.ink in your browser and can get started right away.

The app works in most modern mobile browsers. bun.ink is optimised for **Chrome**, because Chrome has particularly good support for Progressive Web Apps and is very widespread on Android devices. At the same time, access stays low-barrier: anyone with a supported mobile browser can use bun.ink without going through a traditional installation.

## The feel of an app without a traditional installation

Even so, bun.ink shouldn't feel like just another website. Dedicated icons are provided for smartphones and tablets, so you can launch bun.ink straight from your home screen as a shortcut. This principle follows the idea of **Progressive Web Apps (PWA)**: a web app is delivered through the browser, but can be reached on your device just like an app.

The benefit is a practical one: you tap the icon, land directly in your writing environment, and don't have to type in an address or hunt for a bookmark every time.

## Adding bun.ink to your home screen

Depending on your device and browser, the menu item is named slightly differently. But the basic principle stays the same: open the page, call up the browser menu, add to home screen or install.

### iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open bun.ink in **Safari**.
2. Tap the share icon in the Safari bar.
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Check the suggested name or adjust it.
5. Tap **Add**.
6. The bun.ink icon appears on your home screen and can be moved around like any app icon.

If the option isn't immediately visible, it may be further down the action list, or you can reveal it by editing the actions.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open bun.ink in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three-dot menu on the right of the address bar.
3. Select **Add to Home screen** or, if available, **Install app**.
4. Follow the steps shown on screen.
5. bun.ink will then appear as an icon on your home screen.
6. From now on, launch bun.ink directly via this icon.

On Android, depending on the website and your device settings, Chrome may distinguish between a simple home screen shortcut and an installable web app. What matters most for you: bun.ink is quicker to reach afterwards and feels considerably closer to an app in everyday use.

## Always up to date, without update stress

One big advantage of this approach is less visible, but very valuable day to day: **updates happen in the background**. New improvements can be rolled out the same day, without you having to download an update from an app store.

So you never have to wonder whether your version of the app is still current. When bun.ink is improved, you automatically get the best available version the next time you load it. That keeps things straightforward and also helps on older devices: as long as your mobile browser is still supported, bun.ink can keep running.

## What's different on your phone

The editor works just the same on a smartphone as it does at your desk — only the controls around it are tailored to the smaller screen:

- **The toolbar stays put.** It doesn't scroll out of view, so the formatting menu and save button are always within reach.
- **One swipe to the sidebar and back.** On a phone, the sidebar with files, GitHub and history sits on top of the text. Instead of scrolling all the way up through your text, you swipe down on the toolbar: that takes you to the sidebar and remembers where you were in the text. A swipe up brings you back there — even from the end of a long chapter.
- **Tabs as a compact list.** Instead of a tab bar, you pick open documents from a list.
- **Suggestions in full screen.** When a [revision arrives as a pull request](/blog/reviews-as-pull-requests), **Show suggestions** opens it across the full width. **Back to the text** returns you to the editor, and tapping a spot in the suggestions jumps straight to its line in the text.

## Writing where the text comes to life

bun.ink doesn't want to put an extra hurdle between you and your text. That's why the mobile browser, the home screen icon and automatic updates all work together: you reach the app quickly, use it on your smartphone and tablet in an adapted interface, and never have to worry about installation or update routines.

That way, the focus stays where it belongs: on the writing.
